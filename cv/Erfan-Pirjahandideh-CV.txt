Selected Independent Projects

Brain Signals Lab — EEG motor imagery · *Completed evaluation · Public dataset* | github.com/erfanpj/brain-signals-lab
• Tested EEG classification of imagined left- vs right-fist movement on public PhysioNet data: 50 participants, 2,245 usable trials, five participant-held-out folds; band and settings chosen on training participants only.
• CSP + LDA reached 61.8% mean accuracy versus a 50.4% majority-class baseline (participant median 57.8%); a reduced EEGNet variant reached 53.9%. Results apply to this evaluated dataset subset, not to EEG decoding in general.
OpsDesk AI — human-reviewed support automation · *Local prototype · Browser simulation* | github.com/erfanpj/opsdesk-ai
• Built a local support-assistance workflow: handbook retrieval (keyword, BM25, dense, hybrid), proposals from a small local language model, human approval and mock actions.
• Editing a proposal cancels its approval; mock execution is idempotent per approval; every step is written to a hash-chained audit log. A FastAPI review UI and an MCP adapter share the same service.
• On 60 held-out fictional tickets, rules produced 37 fully correct proposals versus 25 from the local model, so I kept the rules as the default.
Breast Cancer Classifier Comparison · *Completed evaluation · Not a clinical tool* | github.com/erfanpj/Breast_Cancer_Project
• Leakage-aware rework with nested cross-validation; on 143 held-out samples a tuned MLP (F1 0.950) matched an untuned logistic-regression baseline (0.951).
Exploratory Alzheimer’s Data Analysis — Julia · *Educational analysis · Synthetic data* | github.com/erfanpj/exploratory_alzheimers
• Reproducible descriptive-analysis workflow with validation, summary tables and 49 automated tests on 2,149 synthetic records. The data does not represent real patients; the results are not medical findings.
